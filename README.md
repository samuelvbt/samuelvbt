### About me

I build backend services and data pipelines for a company that runs on large volumes of operational data.

My day-to-day is **Java backend development**, **SQL Server data warehouses**, **ETL with SSIS**, **REST API integrations** and customizations on the **Sankhya ERP**. Most of my recent work has been designing dimensional data warehouses — sales, supply chain and e-commerce — and the ETL that keeps them trustworthy.

I joined Constance in 2022 and spent my first years in HR and Strategic Management before moving into IT, going from Junior Systems Analyst to Junior Developer to my current role as a mid-level developer. That detour is the reason I tend to start from the business problem rather than from the code.

**Education** — Postgraduate degree in Software Engineering (PUC Minas), B.Tech in Systems Analysis and Development (Estácio), Bachelor's in HR Management. I work with Scrum daily and I'm studying English with a focus on tech.

My goal is to keep growing as a backend engineer and to work on data-intensive systems with international teams.
